# 003a — Which phrase objective recovers the known late-layer phrase representation?

Status: **draft, 2026-09-17.** Awaiting agreement. Inserted before 003 after 002 showed the log-probability
conditional objective saturates. Runs after 001d (activations) is regenerated.

## 1. Question

For multi-token names whose latent representation is known to exist (001/001f: a direction fit on emission
states separates latent two-hop states at AUC 0.96–1.00 at L52–62), which corpus-averaged gradient
objective produces a vector that (a) is reliable across contexts, (b) reads that latent representation, and
(c) carries information beyond the constituent-token J coordinates?

## 2. Why now

002 established that the implementation is exact at a fixed graph shape, and that `v_cond` under a
log-probability target vanishes when the prefix determines the suffix (‖v_cond‖/‖g₁‖ = 0.001–0.08 on real
phrases; nulls larger and more reliable). 001f established that the representation is present. So the open
variable is the target functional. 003 as drafted would spend ~15k backwards on the saturating objects;
003a spends ~3k to choose the object and the baseline first.

## 3. Objects (per phrase token w_i at its teacher-forced position t_i = t′+i−1; all at batch size 1)

| object | per-token target | note |
|---|---|---|
| `logp` | log P(w_i \| c, w_<i) | 002's object; control expected to saturate |
| **`lin`** | q_{w_i}ᵀ h_{62,t_i}, q = (1+γ)⊙W_U[w_i] | the J-compatible functional at the phrase-conditioned position; m=1 recovers 002's `v_lin` up to target aggregation |
| `logit` | z_{w_i}(t_i) through the last block and final norm | actual output logit; differs from `lin` (002: cos 0.78–0.93, norm ratio 0.2) |
| **`odds`** | z_{w_i} − log Σ_{j≠w_i} exp z_j | log-odds; non-saturating, shift-invariant |

For each object: `v_seq = Σ_i g_i`, `v_cond = Σ_{i>k} g_i` (k = family prefix length), gradients reduced two
ways and stored per context. **Pre-registered reduction:** the vector read at the final pre-phrase position,
`at_tprime`, is the **primary** lens vector for the latent benchmark (the latent readout is at the final
prompt token, the same kind of position); the mean over valid source positions ≤ t′ (`source_mean`, the
J-style global analogue) is the secondary arm, reported alongside. No `v_PB` in 003a.
Single-token positive control: the first-token object `g₁^{lin}` (source-mean reduction) is compared to the
released J row `J_ℓᵀq_{w₁}` at L36+; expect high but not unit cosine (single target lag vs all lags, 20 vs 25
contexts). "general relativity" is not used (1 natural occurrence in pile-10k). Everything is computed
with `per_token_fast` (retained graph) at batch 1; nothing is compared across graph shapes. Comparisons to
released J and J-sum are restricted to L36+ (002: shape floor relerr ≤ 0.03 there).

## 4. Data

**Phrases (6).** Latent-evaluable, with siblings (001 families): New Zealand, South Korea, San Diego,
North Carolina. Emission-only, in the template vocabulary (for the 2×2 and reliability): ice cream, credit
card. All 2 tokens, prefix length 1, ≥ 40 emission-natural contexts.
Dropped from the suggestion list: general relativity (1 natural occurrence in pile-10k).

**Fitting contexts, two regimes, 20 each per phrase, split-half by context id.**
- *natural*: emission-natural contexts from `contexts.json` (text immediately before the phrase; ≤ 128 tokens).
- *generic-medium*: pile-10k positions (disjoint from natural docs and from the Σ corpus) where the phrase
  is teacher-forced after an arbitrary position; binned by whole-phrase mean surprisal −(1/m) log P(w|c);
  the medium bin (between the 33rd and 67th percentile of surprisal over 500 sampled positions per phrase).

**Nulls (surprisal-matched).** For each real phrase, two 2-token non-name bigrams mined from pile-10k whose
suffix log-prob under the natural regime lies within ±0.3 nats of the phrase's mean (002: New Zealand −0.01,
South Korea −0.50, San Diego −0.44, ice cream −0.35), plus one improbable control (the 002 junk pair). Nulls
get the same objects and the same reliability metrics; they are a reference, not a class.

**Evaluation activations.** 001's latent two-hop activations (`outputs/001/acts.pt`, regenerated by 001d)
at the final prompt token; emission-natural held-out contexts (the 20 not used for fitting); 10k null pile
activations (`outputs/001/null_acts.pt`).

**Layers.** L36, L44, L52, L56, L60 (L36 as the one mid-band point; L44+ where shape noise is small).

## 5. Baselines

- First-token J: `J_ℓᵀq_{w_1}` (chance within family by construction).
- J-sum / J-mean over constituents (001's bar at late layers: within-family AUC 0.83–0.86 on answer-matched pairs).
- **Constituent-J subspace ceiling (new):** features x(h) = [J_ℓᵀq_t · h for every constituent token t of
  every family member] (r = 5–8 features); cross-validated LDA/logistic classifier on those features only,
  grouped leave-one-cue-out on latent, 5-fold on emission. This bounds what any recombination of ordinary
  token atoms can do.
- Full-residual probe (001's (Σ+λI)⁻¹Δμ, same folds): the linear ceiling.
- Released template row t_w where it exists (New Zealand, ice cream, credit card).

## 6. Metrics (per object × regime × layer)

1. **Reliability:** split-half cosine of `v_cond` and `v_seq` as a function of context count (5, 10, 20).
2. **Norm:** ‖v_cond‖/‖g₁‖, with the phrase's mean suffix surprisal alongside (002 showed these covary).
3. **Readout on latent states (fixed direction, no refit):** pairwise AUC of ⟨v_cond, h⟩ between the phrase's
   latent contexts and each sibling's, and within-family argmax accuracy using each member's own `v_cond`
   (for the four geographic phrases, using their siblings' vectors computed the same way: 4 + 3 + 3 + 2 extra
   phrases at natural regime only, 20 contexts each). Emission held-out AUC likewise.
4. **Beyond constituents:** AUC of `v_cond` vs the constituent-J ceiling and vs J-sum on the same pairs; and
   the residual test: cosine of `v_cond` to its projection onto span{J_ℓᵀq_t : constituent t}; a `v_cond`
   that lies inside that span is a recombination, not a new direction.
5. **Cross-regime cosine:** natural vs generic-medium `v_cond` per object.
6. **Coverage and accuracy reported separately.** A phrase whose split-half cosine at 20 contexts is < 0.9
   counts as a failure (AUC 0.5) in the headline number; the conditional-on-stable number is reported second.
7. **Positive control:** `v_seq^{lin}` for a single-token word on the same contexts vs 002's `v_lin` row
   (target-aggregation differs: single position vs all t′ ≥ t; report cosine, expect > 0.95 at L44+).

## 7. Pre-registered readings

- `logp` ≈ 0 / unreliable, `odds` or `lin` stable and reads latent states above J-sum → saturation was the
  failure; the stable object becomes 003's phrase-J.
- `lin` reads, `logit` does not (or vice versa) → the last block / final norm matters; report which, and
  understand before calling either "J-lens".
- All non-saturating objects reliable but **inside the constituent-J span** and no better than the
  constituent ceiling → phrase gradients are recombinations of token atoms; 003 is reframed as a labelling /
  composition problem (template, trie, subspace), not a new-direction problem.
- Full probe ≫ constituent ceiling, and a non-saturating `v_cond` closes part of that gap → genuine
  phrase-level linear information that a causal object recovers; full 003 proceeds with that object.
- All three non-saturating objects ≈ 0 or unreliable at 20 contexts, latent states still separable by the
  probe → the phrase representation is linearly readable but not aligned with any verbalization gradient of
  its constituent sequence; pivot to template / word-head / subspace methods for the causal leg.
- Full probe ≈ constituent ceiling → the families do not contain phrase-level information beyond tokens;
  003 needs different items (multi-token concepts whose tokens are individually uninformative).

## 8. Kill criterion

No non-saturating object reaches split-half cosine ≥ 0.9 at 20 contexts on any of the four geographic
phrases at any layer ≥ L44 → phrase-J as a corpus-averaged direction is not viable on this model at this
context budget; write the negative.

## 9. Cost

Fitting: 6 phrases + ~12 sibling phrases (natural only) + 12 nulls, × 20 contexts × 2 regimes (6 phrases) or
1 regime (siblings, nulls), × 2 tokens × 4 objectives on a retained graph ≈ 4–5k scalar backwards ≈ 1–1.5 h
at ~1 s each. Surprisal binning: ~500 teacher-forced forwards per phrase ≈ 10 min. Evaluation: CPU on cached
activations. 001d rerun (14 min) is the prerequisite.
