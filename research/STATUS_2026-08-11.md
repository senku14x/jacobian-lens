## 3. A1 — per-ε fits (bug 1 fixed) — the margin collapses

Pooling all scales into one accumulator weights each sample by ‖δ‖², so ε=1.0 samples carried 10⁴×
the weight and "T_sec" was really T_sec(ε≈1). Fit per scale:

**Held-out D4, ε=0.2, cos with true Δ_odd (summed), 32 held-out prompts, 252 deltas:**

| layer | I | J̄ | R̄ | J+0.3I | T_sec^D4 | T_sec^D4+D1 | T_RC | **J_loc** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| L16 | .072 | .108 | .124 | .115 | — | — | — | **.478** |
| L31 | .130 | .181 | .190 | .189 | .256 [.19,.33] | .283 [.22,.36] | .315 | **.600 [.57,.63]** |
| L46 | .288 | .348 | .354 | .356 | .398 [.32,.47] | .417 [.33,.49] | .465 | **.754 [.74,.77]** |

The earlier +0.257 margin was an artifact of scale mixing. `T_sec − J̄` at ε=0.2 is now **+0.075
(L31)** / **+0.050 (L46)**, with CIs that overlap J̄'s.

**Cross-ε (fit ↘ / eval →), L31:** an operator fit at ε=1.0 predicts ε=0.2 effects (**.400**)
*better* than the operator fit at ε=0.2 itself (.283). Reading: the small-ε fits are noise-limited,
not that large-ε structure transfers down. Worth keeping — it means "fit at the scale you test" is
not automatically right when SNR varies with ε.

**J_loc is the story at every scale ≤ 0.2**, and loses only at ε=1.0 (L31: T_sec .611 vs J_loc
.345), which is the expected failure of a linear extrapolation from ε=0.01.
