## 1. Bottom line

**The Phase-A negative on the secant does not survive the broadened bank — the fit was
identifiability-starved, not wrong-in-kind.** Widening the natural-delta family 4.2× (7 222 →
30 096 deltas, D4 effective rank 117 → 555) roughly doubled `T_sec`'s held-out accuracy and moved
it from "inside J̄'s CI" to "CI-clear above J̄ at all three layers."

**But that is the same-distribution axis, which was never the gate — and all three span-immune
checks re-run on the broadened bank still go against the secant, with margins that did not
shrink.** Stein: `T_sec − SG-J` = −0.306 (L16) / −0.469 (L31). In-span: `T_sec − J̄` = −0.119 (D1)
/ −0.199 (D2), *wider* than on the narrow bank. Behavioural M2 on the refit operators:
`T_sec − J̄` = −0.861, indistinguishable from a matched-norm random direction. **G-SECANT: FAIL.**

**Context-averaging remains the largest single effect and is now measured much more precisely,
and it shrank at depth**: `J_loc/J̄` = 4.34 / 3.17 / **1.95** at L16/L31/L46, with the L46 CI now
entirely *below* 2×.

**The conditional low-rank model (C2) is a negative where it matters.** Even with oracle
coefficients, rank-16 recovers 23% / 40% / 85% of the `J̄ → J_loc` gap at L16/L31/L46 — i.e. it
works well only at the layer where the gap is smallest and there is least to explain.

## 2. What changed vs. 2026-08-11, and why

Two bugs in the *verification* layer, both now fixed, neither affecting a science number:

1. **A0's `counts` assert was a hard-coded expectation from the original bank** (`total=7222`).
   The broadened bank tripped it and reported FAIL on all three layers. Replaced with a
   manifest-driven check — the real integrity question is "does the file contain what the builder
   recorded writing", which is bank-independent.
2. **A0's duplicate check used an 80-dim rounded signature** and flagged 1–4 collisions per layer.
   Inspecting them: *every* one is a D2 row sharing a `tag`. D2 is `v_t = Jᵀ(γ⊙u_t)`, a function
   of token id and layer only, so identical directions at different sites are the family's
   definition. The assert now permits exactly that and rejects any duplicate inside D4/D1, plus a
   full-precision exact-duplicate check. **Both banks PASS** under the corrected rule — including
   the original, which is the regression check that the fix did not merely loosen the test.

One real bug in the analysis layer:

3. **`A2_A5_model.py` reconstructed base prompts by re-running the *original* builder's template
   code.** Against the broadened bank the base indices addressed the wrong prompts and it crashed
   on a position index (`index 101 out of bounds`). A crash was the lucky outcome; the same defect
   could have produced quiet nonsense. Fixed at the root: `B2_patch_bank_bases.py` writes
   `base_ids` into every bank pack and **verifies by re-measurement** — recompute
   `h_ℓ(x′)[p] − h_ℓ(x)[p]` with the model and require cos > 0.999 against the stored delta
   (observed: **1.000000** on every checked row, both banks, all layers). Consumers now read
   `base_ids` from the pack and assert its presence.

## 4. A1 on the broadened bank — the secant margin returns

Held-out D4, ε=0.2, cos with true Δ_odd (summed), **120 held-out base prompts, n=1 725** (was 32
prompts / 252 deltas).

| layer | I | J̄ | R̄ | J+0.3I | T_sec^D4 | T_sec^D4+D1 | T_RC | J_loc |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| L16 | .069 | .110 | .124 | .116 | .394 | **.404** [.37,.44] | .416 | .479 |
| L31 | .132 | .188 | .195 | .195 | .353 | **.368** [.33,.40] | .385 | .597 |
| L46 | .315 | .380 | .383 | .388 | .511 | **.522** [.49,.56] | .546 | .742 |

`T_sec − J̄` = **+0.294 / +0.180 / +0.142** (L16/L31/L46), CI-clear at every layer.
On the narrow bank the same quantity was +0.075 / +0.050 with overlapping CIs.

Interpretation, held at *observation* level: **the secant's apparent quality scales with the
breadth of the family it is fitted on.** That is the signature of a fit that was
rank-starved rather than of a real operator — and it is equally the signature of a real operator
that needs enough data to identify. These two are not distinguished by this table, which is
precisely why the gate is elsewhere.

`T_RC ≈ T_sec + 0.012…0.024` — the R anchor now contributes a small but consistent amount, where
on the narrow bank it contributed nothing (agreement to 4 dp).

**Cross-ε remains the same shape and is now sharper**: an operator fit at ε=1.0 predicts ε=0.2
effects better (L31 .454, L46 .615) than the operator fit at ε=0.2 itself (.368, .522). Small-ε
fits are noise-limited. "Fit at the scale you test" is not automatically right when SNR varies
with ε.

**J_loc still wins at every ε ≤ 0.2 and loses at ε=1.0** (L31: T_sec .699 vs J_loc .361) — the
expected failure of a linear extrapolation taken far from its expansion point.

## 6. A4 / M2 — the behavioural, span-immune gate

Direction-identification by ablation on 40 `probe-swap` items, judge-free Δ log-prob of the
correct answer token, same layer band for every operator, matched-norm random controls.

**Band {16, 31, 46}:**

| operator | Δ logp | 95% CI | top-1 kept on pile |
|---|---|---|---|
| **J̄** | **+0.856** | [+0.437, +1.354] | .977 |
| R̄ | +0.587 | [+0.290, +0.975] | .980 |
| logit lens | +0.022 | [+0.007, +0.039] | .980 |
| T_RC | +0.006 | [−0.006, +0.021] | .977 |
| T_sec^D4+D1 | −0.002 | [−0.013, +0.008] | .961 |
| random | −0.005 | [−0.008, −0.001] | — |
| T_sec^D4 | −0.029 | [−0.042, −0.018] | .973 |

`T_sec^D4+D1 − J̄` = **−0.858** [−1.354, −0.438] CI-clear. `T_sec^D4 − random` = −0.025
CI-clear, i.e. the D4-only secant's probe direction is *worse than a random direction* at
removing the answer. No operator wins by breaking the model (top-1 retention .96–.98 throughout).

`J̄ − R̄` = +0.269 [+0.073, +0.554] CI-clear — **J beats R here**. Scope: this is Δ log-prob on
40 single-token probe-swap items at a 3-layer band, not the R-lens post's protocol (30 multihop
questions, first-half-of-layers band, 8 samples, autorater). It is not a replication attempt and
does not contradict the post; it does mean the R-anchor's value is not established on this metric.

**This is the single most damaging result for the secant**, because ablation never asks an
operator to predict a held-out δ, so the identifiability confound cannot explain it.
**Caveat that keeps it provisional: these are the narrow-bank operators.** The re-run on B2
operators is queued.

## 7. C2 — conditional / low-rank deviation model

Shared-subspace sketch `Y_i = J_loc(x_i)·S`, S = top-192 eigenvectors of pooled C_δδ, 56 sites,
29 held out, eval directions inside span(S), ε=0.2.

Variance explained by the deviation PCA over sites:

| layer | top-1 | top-4 | top-16 | top-32 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L16 | .148 | .305 | **.606** | .839 |
| L31 | .061 | .192 | **.525** | .799 |
| L46 | .054 | .172 | **.495** | .787 |

Fraction of the `J̄ → J_loc` gap recovered, **with oracle coefficients** (fitted on the held-out
site's own deviation — an upper bound, not a usable model):

| layer | rank 1 | rank 8 | rank 16 | rank 32 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L16 | 17.7% | 19.4% | **23.2%** | 25.6% |
| L31 | 30.3% | 36.1% | **40.0%** | 40.4% |
| L46 | 58.2% | 73.7% | **85.2%** | 89.8% |

**G-CONDVIABLE, as literally pre-registered (top-16 > 50% variance): 2/3 pass** — but 0.525 and
0.495 straddle the threshold, so the criterion is doing no work. **The direct measurement it was
a proxy for fails**: at L16 and L31 — the layers where context-averaging costs the most (4.34×,
3.17×) — a rank-16 oracle model recovers under half the gap, and rank-32 buys almost nothing over
rank-16 (+2.4 pts, +0.4 pts). Recovery is **anti-correlated with need**: the model works at L46,
where the gap is 1.95× and J̄ is already at .38.

Reading it as a FAIL on the strength of the direct measurement rather than the proxy, per the
"ambiguous = FAIL" rule. The deviation `J_loc(x) − J̄` is real and large but is **not
low-rank-shared across inputs** at the layers where it matters. Predicted (non-oracle)
coefficients were not run: an oracle that already fails bounds them.
